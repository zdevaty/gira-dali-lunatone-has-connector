Gateway and bus

Clock and location

The gateway runs its own schedules by its own clock, in its own time zone. Sunrise and sunset schedules also need its location.

Driver polling

How often the gateway asks each driver for its status and level. This is what keeps the badges on the Devices page current. Each query is two frames on the bus and two lines in the capture; the knobs and this app do not need it.

Runs on the gateway by itself

Automations stored on the gateway. They act without Home Assistant or this app, so a light they change is not explained by any knob or any Home Assistant call. Read-only here.

Zones

Setup history

A copy of the gateway's setup -- devices, groups, scenes, zones, automations, polling, clock settings -- saved nightly and after every change from this app, but only when something differs. Levels and lamp status are left out: they change by themselves.
